## About Us
The name Kaimei comes from the Japanese term “解明” which means to clarify, elucidate, or to solve/reveal something (i.e. a blackbox) through investigation. The name captures the very essence of what Kaimei AI intends to be: a platform/service that allows the unraveling and elucidation of large complex black-box AI models that, after training, are just a large array of numbers with no visibility, interpretability, or understanding into what the AI has learned, how it has learned it, its inherent strengths and weaknesses, vulnerabilities, etc. 

As AI rapidly evolves and becomes smarter and more powerful, it is ever so critical to understand the knowledge that AI has learned and to deeply understand the mechanics behind every deployed intelligent AI model to minimize the risks of unintended AI actions, and to ultimately prevent AI from going out of control. 

The purpose of Kaimei is to build a generalizable and scalable platform/API/service using algorithms developed from extensive AI interpretability/explainability research that allows institutions, firms, and individuals to analyze their trained AI models’ knowledge and structure, to understand and uncover (to “kaimei”) the meanings and patterns hidden within the AI’s black-box-like brain, represented as a large complex array of numbers.
